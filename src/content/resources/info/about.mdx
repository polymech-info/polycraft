We are builders, engineers, and problem-solvers dedicated to turning complex ideas into practical, scalable solutions. Our work spans from custom machinery design to specialized software development for manufacturing, automation, and content creation.

Rooted in open-source hardware and a belief in accessible innovation, we create tools that are not only powerful and efficient but also designed for collaboration and long-term maintainability. From industrial automation systems to recycling and material research, we thrive at the intersection of engineering and sustainability.

Beyond building, we also provide documentation, training, and support—ensuring our technology is not just delivered, but understood and improved upon.
